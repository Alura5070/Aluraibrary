About Us

St Andre House of Formation is a Catholic formation house located in Jinja City, Uganda, dedicated to intellectual, spiritual and human formation.

This Digital Library was created to support seminarians, formators, students, and all seekers of knowledge by providing free access to thousands of Catholic and academic books.

Our Mission: Evangelization through education and free access to knowledge.

Our Motto: Veritas et Caritas - Truth and Charity
